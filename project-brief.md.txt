# My project brief

## The question

Which settlements in Agbado-Oke-Odo, Alimosho LGA sit in low-lying land within 200 metres of the Ilo-Awela River?

## The data I need

* Ward/LGA boundaries – GRID3 – [https://data.grid3.org](https://data.grid3.org) – GeoPackage
* Settlement extents – GRID3 – [https://data.grid3.org](https://data.grid3.org) – GeoPackage
* Watercourses – OSM via QuickOSM – extracted for Agbado-Oke-Odo
* Elevation – Copernicus DEM 30 m – [https://portal.opentopography.org](https://portal.opentopography.org)
